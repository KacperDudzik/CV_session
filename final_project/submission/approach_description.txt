ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 fine-tuned on the MILK10k dermoscopic images only (one image per lesion; no external images, no metadata, no clinical close-ups). Images are resized to 224x224 and normalised with ImageNet mean/std; training uses random horizontal and vertical flips. The last two residual stages (layer3, layer4) and a new 11-way linear head are trained with Adam (lr 1e-4, batch 64, 20 epochs, best epoch kept by validation macro-F1); earlier layers stay frozen. Class imbalance is handled by sampling training lesions with probability inversely proportional to class frequency. The recipe was selected on a lesion-level validation split among 34 configurations x 3 seeds (frozen and partially fine-tuned ResNet-18, ViT-B/16, VGG-16, DenseNet-121, EfficientNet-B0 and a small CNN, with learning-rate, epoch, dropout, weight-decay, augmentation and class-balancing variants). For the submission, one threshold per class was tuned on validation and the softmax probabilities were rescaled so that each threshold maps to 0.5. Local held-out estimate of the leaderboard metric: 0.40. External resources: ImageNet-1k pretrained weights from torchvision only. Course project, EADA Computer Vision 2026.
